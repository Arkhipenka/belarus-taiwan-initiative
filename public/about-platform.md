# Belarus–Taiwan & East Asia Platform

An independent initiative connecting Belarus, Taiwan and East Asia through research, education and cultural exchange. The platform explores democratic transformation, historical memory and civic participation, and creates space for dialogue across borders.

## Content

- Research articles and summaries, with sources and original-language information on each page.
- News and reports about the platform's activities.
- Lectures, discussions, cultural encounters and conference participation.
- Explorations of the geography, everyday culture and historical memory of Belarus and Taiwan.

The site is available in English (/en), Belarusian (/by), Russian (/ru), Polish (/pl) and Traditional Chinese (/zh).
